Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

UZIP Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website for everyone, and we welcome feedback that helps us identify areas for improvement.

1. Our Commitment

We aim to design and maintain our website in a way that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability, and we work toward conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, where practicable.

2. Measures We Take

  • Structuring content with clear headings and readable text
  • Providing text alternatives for meaningful images where applicable
  • Designing forms and interactive elements to be usable with a keyboard
  • Maintaining reasonable color contrast between text and backgrounds
  • Testing our site periodically as part of ongoing improvements

3. Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some parts of our website may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and address these limitations as part of our ongoing accessibility efforts.

4. Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers, or if you need assistance accessing any content or feature on our site, please contact us at info@jwclab.com, and we will make reasonable efforts to respond and assist promptly.

5. Alternative Access

If you are unable to access any content or complete any transaction on our website due to a disability, please contact us directly and we will work with you to provide the information or service you need through an alternative method.
