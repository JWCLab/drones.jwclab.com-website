Cookie Preferences

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how UZIP Inc. ("UZIP," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies on our website, and the choices available to you.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to support certain site features.

2. Cookies We Use

Our website currently uses the following category of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: When you log in to your account, we store a secure authentication token as a cookie in your browser. This cookie is essential for keeping you logged in and allowing you to access account features. Without this cookie, login functionality cannot work.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or analytics cookies on this website.

3. Managing Cookies

Because we only use strictly necessary cookies for login functionality, there is no separate preference toggle required to use our site — simply avoid logging in if you do not wish for this cookie to be set. You can also control or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Please note that blocking the necessary login cookie will prevent you from staying logged in to your account.

4. Changes to This Policy

If our use of cookies changes in the future — for example, if we introduce analytics or advertising cookies — we will update this page accordingly, along with the "Last updated" date above.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at info@jwclab.com.
